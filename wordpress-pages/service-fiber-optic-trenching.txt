<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-text-align-center has-deep-atlantic-color has-text-color" style="font-size:2.5rem;font-weight:900">The Safest Way to Trench for Fiber in Virginia</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"align":"center","style":{"spacing":{"margin":{"bottom":"2rem"}}}} -->
<p class="has-text-align-center" style="margin-bottom:2rem">Virginia's broadband expansion is accelerating — and the number one cause of costly project delays is mechanical trenching equipment hitting existing buried utilities. Beach Hydrovac uses hydro excavation to cut precise, narrow trenches for fiber optic conduit without damaging gas lines, electric cables, water mains, or existing fiber.</p>
