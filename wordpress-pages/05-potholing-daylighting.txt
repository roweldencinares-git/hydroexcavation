<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-deep-atlantic-color has-text-color" style="font-weight:900">What is Potholing / Daylighting?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"fontSize":"1.125rem"}}} -->
<p style="font-size:1.125rem"><strong>Potholing</strong> (also called <strong>daylighting</strong>) is the process of safely exposing underground utilities by using high-pressure water and a powerful vacuum system — without mechanical tools that could damage what's buried below.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For contractors, engineers, and municipalities across Virginia Beach and Hampton Roads, potholing is the safest way to verify the exact location, depth, and condition of buried utilities before any major excavation begins.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At Beach HydroVac, we perform potholing as part of a complete <strong>"Map First, Dig Second"</strong> workflow in partnership with <strong>AIM Locating</strong> (Advanced Infrastructure Mapping) — giving you the full picture before a shovel ever hits the ground.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
